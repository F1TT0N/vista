What it sends

Everything the headset needs to show the scene the way Blender shows it, prepared for you, so there is nothing to export by hand.

🎨

Materials, baked

Procedural and node materials are baked to textures on a copy of the scene, so they look right in VR. Your file is never touched.

💡

Lights and sky

Sun, point, spot and area lamps with their colour and strength, plus the World HDRI, turned and as bright as you set it in Blender.

🎥

Cameras as viewpoints

Every camera becomes a place to stand. Step from one to the next and look around at real scale.

🎞️

Animation and shape keys

Plays as it does in Blender. Turn on Sync timeline and scrubbing in Blender moves the headset with you.

🔀

Variants

Put options in collections, such as oak or walnut or day and night, and flip between them in the headset.

🖼️

Renders

Send the finished render too. Panoramic renders open all around you as a 360° image.

🔍

Finds the headset

No IP address to type. Press Find and every Quest on your Wi-Fi with Blender Bridge started shows up. Send to one or to several.

✅

Checks first

Before sending, Check Scene tells you what will arrive differently: too many triangles, how much texture memory, which materials get baked, a tilted sky.

🗂️

Keeps versions tidy

See what you have sent to the headset, add notes, show an older version again or delete the ones you no longer need.

Install and connect

Three minutes, once. After that it is one button.

Step 1

Install in Blender

  1. Download the add-on above.
  2. In Blender: Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions.
  3. Top-right ⌄ menu › Install from Disk… and pick the file.

Or simply drag the download button from this page into Blender.

Step 2

Open Vista on the Quest

  1. Put the headset on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
  2. In Vista Pictures VR, go to Home.
  3. Under Blender Bridge press START.
Step 3

Send

  1. In Blender's 3D view press N and open the Vista tab.
  2. Press Find and your headset appears in the list.
  3. Press Send to Quest, then Open in VR in the headset.
🔄

Automatic updates

Let Blender keep the add-on up to date: Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions › Repositories › + › Add Remote Repository, and paste:

https://f1tt0n.github.io/vista/blender/index.json
🥽

Get it from the headset

The add-on is also inside Vista. When the computer and the Quest are on the same Wi-Fi network, press START under Blender Bridge, open the address the headset shows in your PC browser and click Blender add-on in the top right corner.

Questions

Find does not show my headset.

Check that the Quest and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi network and that you pressed START under Blender Bridge. Some routers keep guest networks apart, and some firewalls block the search. In that case press + and type the address the headset shows, such as http://192.168.1.20:8080.

Does my scene go through the internet?

No. Blender sends it straight to the headset over your own network. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there is no account. See the privacy policy.

Does it change my .blend file?

No. Exporting and baking happen on a temporary copy of the scene in the background, while you keep working.

I set a password on the headset.

Open the Password panel under the Vista tab in Blender and enter the same user name and password.

What does it cost?

The add-on is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It needs Vista Pictures VR on your Meta Quest.
