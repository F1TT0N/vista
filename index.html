What it does

Built for the way you actually use a headset, not for the way a file manager works on a desktop.

🌐

Step inside the picture

Open anything in a full immersive space. Stand inside a 360 sky, orbit a model at any scale, and see it lit by the environment around it.

🎛️

Make it yours

Key light, ambient, reflections and shadows on real sliders. Nine colour grades, sharpness and clarity. Assign a different texture from your own library.

⚡

No waiting

Thumbnails are built in the background at startup and cached, so scrolling stays smooth even through folders of 8K skies. Models show their shape the moment the geometry is read.

🪟

Your room, still there

Passthrough on an opacity slider. Fade your own room in behind whatever you are looking at, all the way from solid to gone.

🔎

More to look at, built in

Search Poly Haven without leaving the app and pull down skies, models and textures at the resolution you choose. Everything there is free and public domain.

🗂️

Wherever your files are

Headset storage, USB drives, SMB and network shares, Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive — all in the same browser.

Nothing leaves the headset

No account, no analytics, no advertising. Your files are read where they sit and are never uploaded. The app reaches the internet only when you ask it to — searching Poly Haven, or opening your own cloud and network storage.
