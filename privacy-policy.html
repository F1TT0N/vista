VISTA PICTURES VR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

The short version. Vista Pictures VR does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your photos, models and HDRI maps stay on your headset unless you deliberately connect a cloud account or a network share, and even then they travel only between your device and the service you chose.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app keeps lives in its own private storage on the headset and is removed when you uninstall it:

  • Thumbnails. Small preview images generated from your files so the browser stays responsive. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Storage → Clear Thumbnail Cache.
  • Preferences. Your sort order, theme, viewing settings and bookmarks.
  • Cloud sign-in tokens. If you connect Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, the access token issued by that service is stored on the device so you do not have to sign in again. It is never sent anywhere except back to that same service.
  • Sample images. The pictures bundled with the app, unpacked on first use.

Files you open

The app needs permission to read files in order to show them. It reads them to display them and for nothing else. Your files are never uploaded, indexed remotely, scanned for content or shared with us or any third party. We never see them: there is no server behind this app that could.

When the app uses the internet

Only when you ask it to, and only in these cases:

  • Poly Haven. If you open the in-app search, the app requests the public asset catalogue from polyhaven.com and downloads the files you choose. These requests carry an identifying User-Agent naming this app and its version, so Poly Haven can recognise the traffic. No information about you is sent.
  • Cloud storage. If you connect an account, the app talks to that provider on your behalf to list and fetch your files.
  • Network shares. If you add an SMB, FTP or WebDAV location, the app connects to that address on your local network.

If you use none of these, the app makes no network connections at all.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • All files access. To browse and open the images, models and HDRI maps stored on your headset. Without it the app has nothing to show.
  • Internet. Only for the three cases above.
  • USB. To read a drive you plug in, if you choose to.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Third-party content

Assets downloaded through the in-app search come from Poly Haven and are released under the CC0 public domain dedication. Their use is governed by Poly Haven’s terms, not ours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect changes that would affect you.
